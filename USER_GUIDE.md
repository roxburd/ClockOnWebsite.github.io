## 💡 Why ClockOn? (The Rationale)

Most time-tracking apps are bloated with complex forms, required tags, project hierarchies, and mandatory cloud logins. **ClockOn is different.**

* **Zero Fuss**: Tap once to clock on, tap once to clock off.
* **Session Integrity**: Hours are stored in guaranteed, non-overlapping **Sessions** (Clock-On / Clock-Off pairs) so your data is always consistent and accurate.
* **100% Free & Ad-Free**: No paywalls, no pop-up ads, no data harvest.
* **Privacy First**: All your data stays safely on your device.

## ⚡ Quick Start (3-Step Onboarding)

1. **Open ClockOn**.
2. Tap **CLOCK ON** when you start work.
3. Tap **CLOCK OFF** when you finish. *That’s literally it!*

## 🎬 Daily Workflow

### 1. Clocking On
* Tap **CLOCK ON** on the main screen.
* Select the account or project you are working on/for.
* **Real-time Live Counter**: A live duration counter appears showing elapsed time (`HH:MM`).

### 2. Switching Accounts
Working on Account A and suddenly need to jump to Account B? Don't clock off and clock on - switch!
* Tap **SWITCH** on the main screen.
* ClockOn automatically clocks you off Account A and clocks you on Account B in a single atomic step — no overlapping sessions or time gaps!

### 3. Clocking Off
* Tap **CLOCK OFF**.
* ClockOn closes your active session, records total hours, and updates your widget/notification bar instantly.

> [!TIP]
> **Forgot to clock on or off earlier?** When clocking on or off, you can adjust the timestamp to the required time!

## 🔔 Persistent Status Bar Notification

While you are clocked on, ClockOn maintains a discreet notification in your Android status bar displaying your active account name.

* **Constant Status Indicator**: A quick glance at your phone's status bar or notification shade confirms that you are currently on the clock and which account you are logging time to.
* **Instant Shortcut to Clock Off**: Tapping the notification takes you straight back to the main screen in ClockOn from anywhere on your device or lock screen, making it effortless to jump in and tap **CLOCK OFF** or **SWITCH** as soon as you finish a task!

## 📱 1-Tap Home Screen Widget

Widgets sit on your device's home screen and provide the quickest access to features you use often.

Add the ClockOn widget to your Android home screen:
1. Long-press your device’s home screen and select **Widgets**.
2. Find **ClockOn** and drag it to your home screen where you want it.
3. Clock on and off with a single tap without even opening the main app!

## 📊 Reports & CSV Export

Need to send timesheets to your manager or export billable hours for invoices?

* **Flexible Views**: Switch between **Weekly** or **Monthly** summaries.
* **Totals vs. Sessions**: View total accumulated hours or inspect individual session entries down to the exact minute.
* **CSV Export**: Select one or more periods, tap the **Action** (`☰`) menu button, and choose **Export** to generate a clean CSV file and share it via Email, Slack, WhatsApp, Google Drive, Excel or other capable channel!

You remain in complete control of where the data goes.

## 📈 Visual Charts & Data Analytics

Want to visualize your work hours at a glance? ClockOn features a comprehensive interactive charting engine:

1. In **Reports**, select one or more periods using the checkboxes.
2. Tap the **Action** (`☰`) menu button at the bottom right and select **Charts**.
3. Choose from **4 distinct view modes**:
   - **Stacked Column**: Displays cumulative booked hours per period (or daily breakdown when viewing a single week or month).
   - **100% Stacked Column**: Normalizes columns to 100% for proportional analysis.
   - **Pie Chart**: Visualizes total time distribution across accounts as percentage slices.
   - **Horizontal Bar Chart**: Compares total booked time per account side-by-side.

### Interactive Chart Features:
* **Combined Key & Filter**: Tap the **Key** button in the top bar to open the legend pop-up. You can toggle accounts on and off to include or exclude them from the charts instantly.
* **Segment Tap Callouts**: Tap any coloured segment on stacked column charts for a callout bubble showing the account name and exact hours/percentage.

## ✏️ Editing & Managing Records

Made a mistake or forgot to log yesterday's hours? ClockOn gives you full control to adjust, add, or remove records in **Reports**:

### 1. Editing Existing Records & Sessions
* Open **Reports** and expand any week or month.
* Tap on any session or record entry.
* Adjust the start time, end time, date, or account in the dialog, then tap **Save**.

### 2. Adding Manual Sessions
* In **Reports**, tap the week/month to expand it and tap the **`+` (Add Session)** button.
* Enter the start time, end time, date, and account to retroactively log a past session.

### 3. Deleting Data
* **Delete Single Session or Record**: Open any session or record by tapping it in Reports, then tap the red **Delete** button.
* **Delete Entire Week or Month**: Tap **Delete Week** or **Delete Month** at the bottom of any week/month block to remove all records for that period.

## ⚙️ Account Management & Custom Colours

Manage multiple jobs, clients, or projects effortlessly:

1. Tap **ACCOUNTS** on the main screen.
2. Rename accounts by tapping on their name and entering a new name.
3. Add new accounts (e.g. *"Acme Corp"*, *"Design Contract"*, *"Overtime"*).
4. **Custom Account Colours**: Personalise each account's colour swatch by tapping the colour circle next to the account name in the Accounts list view, or via Account Details. Use the true colour picker (Hue, Saturation, and Brightness sliders) to choose any custom shade.
5. **Max Hours Limit**: Optionally set an all-time maximum hour limit for any account.
