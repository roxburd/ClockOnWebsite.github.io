## 💡 Why ClockOn? (The Rationale)

Most time-tracking apps are bloated with complex forms, required tags, project hierarchies, and mandatory cloud logins. **ClockOn is different.**

* **Zero Fuss**: Tap once to clock on, tap once to clock off.
* **Session Integrity**: Hours are stored in guaranteed, non-overlapping **Sessions** (Clock-On $\rightarrow$ Clock-Off pairs) so your data is always consistent and accurate.
* **100% Free & Ad-Free**: No paywalls, no pop-up ads, no data harvest.
* **Privacy First**: All your data stays safely on your device.

## ⚡ Quick Start (3-Step Onboarding)

1. **Open ClockOn**.
2. Tap **CLOCK ON** when you start work.
3. Tap **CLOCK OFF** when you finish. *That’s literally it!*

## 🎬 Daily Workflow

### 1. Clocking On
* Tap **CLOCK ON** on the main screen.
* Select the account or project you are working on/for.
* **Real-time Live Counter**: A live duration counter appears showing elapsed time (`HH:MM`).

### 2. Switching Accounts
Working on Account A and suddenly need to jump to Account B? Don't clock off and clock on - switch!
* Tap **SWITCH** on the main screen.
* ClockOn automatically clocks you off Account A and clocks you on Account B in a single atomic step — no overlapping sessions or time gaps!

### 3. Clocking Off
* Tap **CLOCK OFF**.
* ClockOn closes your active session, records total hours, and updates your widget/notification bar instantly.

> [!TIP]
> **Forgot to clock on or off earlier?** When clocking on or off, you can adjust the timestamp to the required time!

## 📲 Home Screen Widget & Notifications

Widgets sit on your device's home screen and provide quicker access to features you use often.

### 📱 1-Tap Home Screen Widget
Add the ClockOn widget to your Android home screen:
1. Long-press your device’s home screen $\rightarrow$ select **Widgets**.
2. Find **ClockOn** and drag it to your screen.
3. Clock on and off with a single tap without even opening the main app!

### 🔔 Persistent Status Bar Notification
While clocked on, ClockOn keeps a discreet, low-importance status notification displaying your active account name.

## 📊 Reports & CSV Export

Need to send timesheets to your manager or export billable hours for invoices?

* **Flexible Views**: Switch between **Weekly** or **Monthly** summaries.
* **Totals vs. Sessions**: View total accumulated hours or inspect individual session entries down to the exact minute.
* **CSV Export**: Tap the **Export** button to generate a clean CSV file and share it via Email, Slack, WhatsApp, Google Drive, or Excel!

You remain in complete control of where the data goes.

## ✏️ Editing & Managing Records

Made a mistake or forgot to log yesterday's hours? ClockOn gives you full control to adjust, add, or remove records in **Reports**:

### 1. Editing Existing Records & Sessions
* Open **Reports** and expand any week or month.
* Tap on any session or record entry.
* Adjust the start time, end time, date, or account in the dialog, then tap **Save**.

### 2. Adding Manual Sessions
* In **Reports**, tap the week/month to expand it and tap the **`+` (Add Session)** button.
* Enter the start time, end time, date, and account to retroactively log a past session.

### 3. Deleting Data
* **Delete Single Session or Record**: Open any session or record by tapping it in Reports, then tap the red **Delete** button.
* **Delete Entire Week or Month**: Tap **Delete Week** or **Delete Month** at the bottom of any week/month block to remove all records for that period.

## ⚙️ Account Management & Max Hours

Manage multiple jobs, clients, or projects effortlessly:

1. Tap **ACCOUNTS** on the main screen.
2. Add new accounts (e.g. *"Acme Corp"*, *"Design Contract"*, *"Overtime"*).
3. **Max Hours Limit**: Optionally set an all-time maximum hour limit for any account.
